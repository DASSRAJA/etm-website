Purpose Before Promotion

Utility & Roadmap

Our goal is to explore practical uses for ETM through careful development, testing and transparent communication.

Our First Utility Direction

Planned — Testnet First

We are exploring a digital-access demonstration where a sample guide or digital resource could be associated with an ETM payment on a supported test network.

This concept is not yet a live public payment service. It requires a correctly configured test network, a deployed test token and application-side transaction verification before it can operate.

Development Roadmap

Stage 1 — Foundation

Basic tests passed

Develop the fixed-supply token contract and verify its basic functions through automated tests.

Stage 2 — Testnet Demonstration

In progress

Configure a test wallet and test network safely. Demonstrate the proposed digital-access flow without real funds.

Stage 3 — Review & Improvement

Planned

Review technical security, user experience, transaction verification and applicable legal requirements.

Stage 4 — Future Utility Assessment

Future consideration

Consider further development only after testing, review and appropriate approvals. No public launch date is promised.

Our Principles

• Practical utility before promotion.

• Transparent information and realistic claims.

• Test carefully before considering real-world use.

• No guaranteed returns or promised token price.
